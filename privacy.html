Dex privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Dex is a personal assistant used by exactly one person, its owner. It is not available to the public, and no one else's account is ever connected to it.

What Dex accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google's sign-in, Dex accesses the owner's own Gmail account to search and read emails, archive them, move them to Spam or Trash, mark them read, and send replies. Every change to the mailbox and every reply requires the owner's approval at the time it happens.

How the data is used and stored

  • Email data is used only to help the owner manage their own mailbox.
  • The access token is stored on the owner's own computer. Emails are not copied into any database or shared with anyone.
  • To understand and write emails, Dex sends the relevant content to the AI model provider it uses (currently Google Gemini), under that provider's terms.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or transferred to any other party.

Dex's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Dex's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
